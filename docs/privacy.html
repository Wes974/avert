Privacy Policy

Avert collects no personal data. There is no Avert server: nothing is sent to its author, or to anyone else.

Effective 30 July 2026.

What Avert reads

To recognise a fraudulent page, the extension has to read it. When a page contains a sensitive field (password, payment card, one-time code, recovery phrase), Avert examines its address, structure, images and a short text excerpt — on the device.

Those are analysed in memory and discarded. They are never stored and never transmitted. On a page with no sensitive field — the overwhelming majority — Avert reads nothing at all.

What leaves the device

Nothing, unless you turn on family mode. The extension has no network capability: every build is checked to make sure the shipped code contains no way to issue a request.

There are no usage analytics, no crash reports, no advertising, no measurement tools and no unique identifier.

Family mode, if you enable it

This mode is off by default. It lets a relative you have explicitly linked be notified. It runs on your own iCloud account through CloudKit; Avert's author has no access to any of it.

If you dismiss a strong warning

Linked relatives receive the date, and the name you gave your device. Nothing else — not the site's address, not the brand involved, not the page content. That boundary is expressed in the code itself and enforced by tests that fail if a field is added.

If you ask a relative for advice

Here, and only here, the site's address is sent — that is the question being asked. Transmitted: the host name, a summary of the analysis, your device name and the date. The request is deleted after 24 hours.

Undoing it

Clearing your device name in Settings stops all sending. Breaking a family link also deletes what that device had published.

Local history, if you enable it

An option, off by default, lets Avert flag a login form on a domain you have never signed in to before. To do so it remembers a list of domain names in the device keychain. That list never leaves the iPhone and can be erased in Settings.

Artificial intelligence

Avert uses the language model built into iOS (Apple Intelligence), which runs on the device. Page content is not sent to any AI service, to Apple or to anyone else.

Children

Avert collects no data, including from minors. Family mode requires an explicit action on both sides of the link, and the person being watched can always see who is watching — a link cannot be set up without their knowledge.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date. The full history is available in the public repository.
